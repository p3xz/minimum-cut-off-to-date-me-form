Test checklist: iPhone Safari and Android Chrome

iPhone Safari

  • Open at 360 to 430px wide: no sideways scroll, title and sheet fit the screen.
  • Tap any input: the page does not zoom in on focus (inputs are 16px).
  • Score buttons sit in one row of 6, at least 44px tall, and the picked one pops with its caption fading in below.
  • Sticky bottom bar always shows Total / 100, the cut-off and how many of 20 are marked, and never covers the footer.
  • Finish all 20: the verdict stamp slams in, the phone vibrates once, and Copy result opens the share sheet.
  • Turn on Reduce Motion in Settings: animations stop, no confetti, no vibration, final scores show instantly.

Android Chrome

  • Rapid-tap the score buttons: no double-tap zoom, the selected button pops to 1.25 and settles.
  • Scroll slowly: each metric row fades and slides up 14px once as it enters the screen.
  • Set the cut-off above 95: the number shakes. Lower it after a full score: it flashes red with the grace-marks warning.
  • Score at or above the cut-off: paper-coloured confetti bursts. Score 15 or more below: zeros fall behind the result card.
  • Tap Rescan under Fig. 1: the scan line sweeps top to bottom in about 2 seconds.
  • Share result: the marksheet preview opens, Share sends the PNG, Download PNG saves it under 1.5 MB.
