    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 14 November 2026

    In plain words

    Nothing you type leaves your browser. No photo is uploaded, no face is scanned, and no answer is sent to a server. Clear it with the Reset button or by closing the tab.

    What is processed, and where

    The site does not upload photos, does not use face recognition and does not send your answers to any server. Name, Instagram handle, age and scores are processed only in your browser. They are used to build the on-screen result and the share card. They are not stored on any server and not sold or shared.

    Storage used on your device

    During one visit, the app may keep your in-progress answers in sessionStorage so Back and Forward navigation does not lose them. That storage lives only in this tab, is wrapped so the app still works if it is unavailable, and is cleared by the Reset button (Pretend this never happened) or by closing the tab. No data is kept on a server.

    Cookies and analytics

    No cookies and no analytics are used. If either is added later, it must be listed here first.

    Sharing your card

    The share card is generated locally on your device. If you share it through your phone, that sharing is governed by the app you choose.

    Third-party resources

    Third-party resources may receive your IP address when loading. The resources used are Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) and cdnjs scripts for html2canvas and canvas-confetti.

    Who this site is for

    The site is for users aged 18 and above only. It is not directed at children, and anyone under 18 is blocked at the age step.

    Your rights

    In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have rights over your personal data.
